# Bon Appétit, Your Majesty!

*A royal tale of cuisine, charm, and courage.*

---

## 📖 Overview

**Bon Appétit, Your Majesty** is a captivating fantasy-drama series where **culinary art meets royal politics**.  
Follow *Princess Celeste*, a reluctant heir who discovers her true passion in the royal kitchen—and the secrets that simmer beneath the palace walls.

> “Food is more than survival—it’s a story served on a plate.”
